LucidPC Remote Support

Three steps to let your support technician connect to your computer.

1

Download & install RustDesk

Click below to download the official RustDesk app. Run the installer when it finishes.

Download RustDesk
2

Copy the support code

Click the button to copy your support code to the clipboard.

3

Import the code into RustDesk

Open RustDesk. Click the three dots ⋮ next to your ID at the top, choose Network, then click Import. Click OK to save.

Tell your support technician the 9-digit ID and password shown by RustDesk. That's it!

Your screen is never shared until you give your technician the ID and password from RustDesk.
